BombVault 10.0.0 is the biggest release so far, with a fully revamped interface. The app opens your servers from any network: when a server doesn't answer at its own address, it loads through the relay. The arrow beside a server's name takes you back to the app's overview in one tap. Servers show up as soon as they answer and open faster. Under Theme, System follows your phone. Signing in with your group's twelve words needs this version and BombVault 10.0.0.
