<objective>
Restore hardening (RESTORE-01): restoring a chosen snapshot intersects the stored positional list with THAT snapshot's recorded `Paths` using longest-prefix mapping — per-path skips instead of mid-loop failure, and a clean pre-teardown abort when nothing maps — so selection churn can never abort a restore after the container has been stopped and removed.

Purpose: the tree makes selection reshaping first-class; today a changed path list fails the restore mid-loop at the adapter AFTER destructive teardown (service.go:6752-6759 fail-fast, reached from orchestrator.go:774 post-Stop/Remove). All mapping and failure resolution moves into the synchronous prepare phase.
Output: `mapRestorePaths` helper + `prepareRestoreForTarget` wiring + orchestrator skip reporting + the restic 0.17 positional spot-checks (locked decision D-16/L12).
</objective>
