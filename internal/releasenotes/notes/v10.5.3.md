BombVault 10.5.3 deletes an entry's backups at every place in one step, keeps copies to Proton Drive going after a failed upload, and keeps every selector on one row.

## ✨ Added

- **Delete backups everywhere.** An entry's menu has **Delete backups everywhere**. It lists each place that holds the entry's backups, at home and at every off-site target, with how many there are, and deletes them all once you type the entry's name. A place set to append-only keeps its backups and says so. The entry and its settings stay, so a container you stopped backing up stays that way. Thanks to @manilx for asking. ([#416](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/416))

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Copies to Proton Drive after a failed upload.** When Proton Drive broke off an upload, it kept the file as a draft and refused every later try, so the off-site copy stopped after about half an hour with "unexpected HTTP response (500)". BombVault now lets rclone replace such a draft. If you set `RCLONE_PROTONDRIVE_REPLACE_EXISTING_DRAFT` on the container as a workaround, you can remove it.

## 🎨 Design

- **Selectors keep to one row.** A selector no longer wraps onto a second row on a phone. It gives up its even widths first, then its words where every option has an icon, and turns into a dropdown where even that does not fit.
- **No colored edges.** Findings and a picked backup row no longer draw a colored bar along their side. The badge says how serious a finding is.

## ⚡ Improved

- **Follows GlimStone 4.3.0.** Full-width rows lift while you hold one, and an item you carry lands where its leading edge is, also in right-to-left languages.

## 🌐 Translations

- **New texts in all 42 languages**, and the documentation pages updated in all 26.
