BombVault 10.7.0 brings what the web interface does to the Android app, each on a narrow route of its own, and runs on ParleyPort 1.3.8, so a member of the group or fortress stays reachable while it reconnects.

## ✨ Added

- **The phone shows and starts ZFS replicas.** Replicate now, Replicate all now, the replica's backups and the ZFS servers on the Instances page show in the app, with the servers' addresses and logins left out.
- **The phone restores Compose stacks, flash plugins and database dumps.** A stack, a plugin or a dump imported into the live database goes back only after BombVault backed up the containers and the project folder, the flash or the container, and nothing is replaced if that backup fails. A dump can also be saved into a new folder in the restore folder, or downloaded at the server's own address when export encryption is on. Without it the app says where to turn it on.
- **The phone restores from an old backup folder.** Under Other sources it opens another instance's repository with its key or recovery kit and the sign-in you type, and puts every entry into a new folder inside the restore folder.
- **The phone settles anomalies.** It acknowledges a finding, marks it as expected, forgets an expectation and sets an entry's sensitivity. The old backups a finding holds stay kept until someone signs in with the password and stops keeping them.
- **The phone runs checks and keeps entries in order.** Check restore and Start test on the Verify tab, Include in schedule, Pause and Resume, Decline and Accept, tags, a ZFS sweep and Discover backups work in the app. Adding an entry stays with the web interface, since the new entry is picked from the server's folders.
- **The phone shows a locked key and what a new release brings.** The banner for a backup key that no longer opens shows in the app, and What's new for a newer release opens after Check for updates.
- **The app's settings gain button labels, the rainbow's two switches, quiet notifications and the manual.** Labels, Reactive mode and Colour rotation follow the first server's look until you set them in the app, and Quiet notifications hold for the phone alone.
- **What's new in the app opens the release notes in the app.** The notes come with the app, so the window opens without a connection too and lists what the release brought, as the web's window does.

## 🎨 Design

- **The words on the restore pictures read in the dark look.** On a picked Replace or Beside target, "Original" and "-restored" were white on the accent colour.
- **The Labels choice in the app's settings fits a phone in every language.** Where the four names don't fit, it shows their symbols alone, as the web's look settings do. "Text and symbol" was cut short.
- **The Local and Off-site switch of the dump list stays inside its card on a phone.** It ran past the card's right edge.

## ⚡ Improved

- **A member that is reconnecting still answers.** A server or app leaves the relay for a moment when it joins a fortress, opens it again, or changes its name or address. A call in that moment, such as its status, an action or a backup asked of it, ended in an error although the member was back right after. BombVault now tries such a member again for up to three seconds, on every call, also when its address turns the connection away in that moment.
- **A renamed server is back on the relay at once.** After a new name or address, the other members listed it as gone for a second or longer.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **An action or backup asked of another member runs once.** When the answer to a direct call got lost on its way back, the call went out again over the relay, so the member could run it twice. It now ends with an error that says the member did not answer.
- **A server that reconnects is no longer listed as away.** The relay could pass on the offline notice of its old connection after the new one was up, and the other members kept it as away until it reconnected again.
- **Leaving a fortress is no longer taken for a removal.** A server that had just left could still get an answer that turned it away, and then said another member had taken it out.
- **A call that is given up keeps the relay connection up.** A call cancelled on its way out, as when its page closes, could drop the relay connection that every other call shares. A fortress closing in that moment could lose its leave, so the other members still listed the server.
- **A run's files can be browsed and restored on a phone's screen.** A container run's details did not list its files at all, because they asked for them under the wrong name. On a paired phone the restore also put the files back where they were, which the phone's session may not do, so it always failed. They now go into a new folder inside the restore folder, as in the restore window, and the question before it says so.
- **The restore window on the phone offers only what the phone can start.** It listed putting ticked files back in place and a new ZFS dataset, which the server turns down for a phone, and switches for the backup first and the ZFS safety snapshot, which the server keeps on there anyway.
- **The phone resolves failures one at a time.** Mark all resolved and resolving a group of several failures ended in an error there, so the phone leaves them out.
- **A folder set's file tree offers only what can be restored.** It let you tick the folders above the set's own path, which the restore then refused.
- **Check restore says on the phone that it runs, and what keeps it back.** Only the button's tip said the check was running, and a phone shows no tips. A check refused during a backup said so in English. The row now says that the restore is being checked, and a refusal names the section being backed up in your language.
- **What's new on the phone leaves out what the phone cannot open.** Two of its points led to the address card and to node copies, which a paired phone does not reach.
- **The password reset says that the phones sign in again by themselves.** Its texts and the docs still told you to switch phone pairing back on, a switch BombVault no longer has. The Android guide also says that saving files from the app needs a sign-in with the password, and an older server that keeps phones out now says what to do.
