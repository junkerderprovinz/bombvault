Settings becomes a column of fourteen pages right beside the sidebar, with a search that appears when you scroll up and takes you to any setting. Pairing gets a page of its own, works without a login password, and keeps two instances on one network paired without a relay. Retention gets a yearly rule, each repository can choose its own compression, and the storage forecast reads free space on SFTP repositories and network shares. A backup can be broken down by folder, a slow backup names what held it back, and a container recreated since its last backup shows what changed. Settings, schedules and repositories carry over as they are: the yearly rule keeps nothing until you set a number, and compression stays at Automatic.

## ✨ Added

### Retention and storage

- **Keep yearly.** The local and off-site policies under **Settings, Retention** and every additional off-site target under **Settings, Off-site** have a yearly rule next to last, daily, weekly and monthly. It keeps one restore point for each of the last N calendar years that has a backup, and the oldest one as well while there are fewer such years. The retention preview and **Prune** follow it too.
- **Compression per repository.** Choose Off, Automatic or Maximum under each backup path and each named repository on **Settings, Storage**, and on each off-site destination on **Settings, Off-site**. Automatic is restic's default and what every repository used so far. Backups, off-site copies and prune write with the setting, and the recovery kit names it, so plain restic can keep writing the same way. It applies to new data, and a repository in restic's old format version 1 ignores it.
- **Free space for SFTP repositories and network shares.** The dashboard's storage forecast shows free space and the weeks until full for SFTP repositories, read over the same SSH login restic uses, and for SMB and WebDAV destinations whose server reports it. S3, B2 and REST repositories cannot report their free space, so they say "Free space unknown" instead of showing nothing. The anomaly check for a disk that will be full soon covers SFTP repositories as well.

### Seeing what a backup holds

- **Size by folder.** In the Backups section of a container, VM or folder set, **Size by folder** shows which folders and files take the space in the newest backup and how much of each the latest backup brought in new or changed, one level at a time. It is read from the repository's index without reading the files, and kept current after each backup once you have opened it.
- **Why a backup was slow.** When a backup takes much longer than usual and one thing was clearly at its limit, the run says so in one line, for example "The target disk disk1 was 98% busy" or "BombVault used 100% of the CPU limit of its container". Otherwise it says nothing.
- **Changed since backup.** A container that was recreated with another image, other ports, variables or volumes since its last backup gets a mark beside its name, on a phone too, and its (i) lists what changed, variables by name only. It is only a note and goes away with the next backup.

## 🎨 Changed

### Settings

- **The eight tabs become fourteen pages** in a column beside the sidebar: General, Look, Storage, Retention, Schedules, Containers, Off-site, Cloud access, Notifications, Integrity, Security, Pairing, Integrations and System. Retention, Containers, Cloud access, Security, Pairing and Integrations used to be parts of other tabs.
- **Every page has its own address**, such as `/settings/offsite`, so a page can be bookmarked and the Back button returns to the page before. Settings opens on the page you had open last. Links to the old tabs and to `/jobs` lead to the matching page.
- **Hold a tile and drag it to reorder the column.** The order is saved with your other display settings. The arrow keys, Home and End move between pages.
- **In a narrow window the column shows only the symbols.** It follows **Labels, Tabs** on the Look page too: with Symbol each tile shows its symbol alone, and with Reactive the name opens under the symbol when you point at a tile. The current page keeps its name, and each tile takes its colour from its place in the column, as the tabs did.
- **Search all settings appears when you scroll up** on a settings page, and goes away when you scroll down or change page, so it does not take room on every page. It searches page names, card titles, the caption beside each control and the explanations behind the (i), but not the values you have set. Picking a result opens its page, scrolls to the card or row and marks it, and when the result is hidden at the moment, because it belongs to the advanced view or to a feature that is off, a note says so.
- **Restart after backup moves to the Containers page**, below the registries.
- **Every settings card fits beside the column on a phone** down to 320 pixels wide. Long titles, paths and rows wrap instead of running off the side.
- **In rainbow mode each path row on the Storage page has its own colour** for its Local/Remote switch and its Browse button, instead of six rows in one colour.
- **Hints and messages that name a place in Settings point at its page**, among them the pairing hint on the Instances page, the off-site retention note, the repository and backup location hints, the MCP sign-in messages, the host SSH notes and errors, the orphan image note, the answer an MCP client gets when its key may only read, the notification about a changed MCP key and the MCP line of the recovery kit. The **Set password** button of the MCP card opens the password field on the Security page.

### Pairing

- **Pairing has its own page in Settings**, always in the column, with the instance name at the end. The Instances page keeps a **Pairing** button that takes you there, and a link to the old Pairing tab under Instances goes there too.
- **Receiver, Instances and Pull**, the three features that work through pairing, are switched on in the Domains card on the General page. Until one of them is on, the Pairing page says so.
- **Generate phrase opens a window with the twelve words** and a Copy button. **Enter phrase** opens a window with the field, twelve numbered slots and a **Paste** button that reads the words from the clipboard. Where the browser does not allow that, for example over plain HTTP, the window says to paste into the field by hand.
- **The member list starts with this instance**, marked This instance, followed by every member with Direct or Via relay. Every row has the same height.
- **A group that stays alone for a minute offers two tiles**: show the words again to enter them on the other instance, or enter the other instance's words and join its group in one step. The two-step hint with Leave group is gone.
- **When the relay can't be reached, the card says so in one line**, with **What to check** for the list and **Check again**.
- **The relay card says No group yet** until this instance has a phrase, since the relay only connects inside a group.
- **Pairing works without a login password.** Generating, entering and showing the phrase no longer need one. Until a password is set, the phrase card warns that anyone who can open the web interface can read the words and, through the group, get the restic password of every instance in it, and its **Set password** button opens the password field on the Security page. With a password set, showing the phrase again still asks for it.

### Instances

- **The Fleet tab is now the Instances tab** and comes first. Each card shows the address that instance answers on.
- **Before there is a group, the tab is one large Pairing tile** that leads to the Pairing page. Once there is one, the **Pairing** button sits below the cards.

### Dashboard, Self-backup and Flash

- **The badges in the protection rows stay on one line.** Their columns take the longest badge on any row, and the schedule text shortens instead. The off-site DR result sits on the line below, next to its button, and in a narrow window each row stacks its badges.
- **The learning caption of anomaly detection sits in the backup card** of Self-backup and Flash, beside the Backup button, instead of on its own above the page.

## ⚡ Improved

- **The MCP server knows the new features.** `get_size_breakdown` answers what takes the space in a backup, `list_items` carries what changed in a container since its backup, `list_runs` names what held a slow backup back, and `get_storage_stats` reports the free space of SFTP repositories and shares and marks the repositories that cannot report it. For the retention guard, a yearly rule on its own counts as keeping one restore point, since that is all it keeps of the current year.
- **The settings export carries the yearly rules and the compression.** A file from an older version leaves the yearly rules and compression of the instance you import it into as they are.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Two instances on the same network without a relay no longer drop out of their group** after a few minutes ([#270](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/270)). An instance found by its address counted as reachable for five minutes, and only opening the pairing or Instances page searched again. Every member address is now asked again every two and a half minutes.
- **A container backed up config only shows its last backup** on the dashboard and in its row instead of Never ([#277](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/277)). Such a backup saves the container's definition and makes no restic snapshot, and the date was taken from snapshots only.
- **The source row of a folder set's backups wraps on a phone.** It ran past the right edge of the screen.
- **On a phone, the backup lists on the Self-backup page and in a container's Backups section keep each date and time on one line.** Depending on the date and the width of the screen, the Restore and Delete buttons could move up beside the time and squeeze it into a narrow column.

## 🌐 Translations

- 67 new strings in all 42 languages for the new pages, cards, hints, messages and the settings search, plus the reworded retention, prune and pairing texts and the corrected Settings hints. Swedish calls off-site Extern throughout.
- The new parts of the features and MCP server pages and the pairing section of the off-site recovery page in all 26 documentation languages. The documentation names the new Settings pages, and the translated pages also carry the sections they were missing: SMB and WebDAV without a host mount, Not backed up, the retention preview, the support bundle, pulling from another instance, the sealed recovery kit and remote primary repositories. The Arabic and Greek pages quote the app's own button and page names, and the Portuguese, Greek and Ukrainian pages keep to one form of address.

## Upgrading

- **Nothing has to be changed.** The database is upgraded on the first start, settings export files from older versions still import, and no setting or environment variable was removed or renamed. restic stays at 0.17.3 and the repositories are left as they are.
- **Bookmarks to the old Settings tabs lead to the matching page**, and one to the old Pairing tab under Instances leads to the Pairing page. The column starts in the default order until you drag a tile.
- **Yearly retention keeps nothing until you set a number, and compression stays at Automatic**, so every repository keeps writing exactly as before.
- **An instance without a login password** shows the password warning on its phrase card until you set one.
- **Size by folder is worked out the first time you open it** for an item, and after each backup of that item from then on. An item you never open it for is never read for it.
