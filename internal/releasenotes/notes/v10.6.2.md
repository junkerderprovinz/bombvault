BombVault 10.6.2 looks after the snapshots your server keeps by itself, and it lets a server keep its backups at home without a red off-site warning.

## ✨ Added

- **Snapshots.** The new **Snapshots** page lists every ZFS, btrfs and VM snapshot on the server, with its size and who took it. You can take, rename, hold and delete them there, and before a delete it tells you how much space comes free. A snapshot that ZFS calls busy shows where it is mounted, and **Free and delete** unmounts it and deletes it while the containers keep running. Disks that are asleep stay asleep unless you ask to read them.
- **Snapshot schedules.** Under **Settings, Snapshots** a schedule takes snapshots of one dataset, subvolume or VM and keeps the newest ones. It only ever removes snapshots it took itself, never held ones and never ones you renamed, and a schedule that fails sends a notification.
- **No off-site copies.** If your backups should stay on the premises, switch this on at the end of **Settings, Storage locations, Off-site copies**. The off-site check then turns gray instead of red, a section with current backups reads **Protected here, no off-site copies**, and a failed or overdue backup still stands out. While it is on, the other off-site settings and the Streaming card stay hidden, and once a section copies off-site the switch goes away. ([#506](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/506), thanks @profusse)

## 🎨 Design

- **Follows GlimStone 4.5.6.** **Add channel** under Notifications opens a window of tiles with each service's logo and a search on top. Adding a container registry works the same way, and its form opens right in that window.

## ⚡ Improved

- **Notifications through TollRoll.** When TollRoll is in your fortress and may send for BombVault, BombVault hands its messages to TollRoll first, so they arrive on TollRoll's channels even where BombVault has no channel of its own.
- **Fresh building blocks.** The Docker client 0.6.3, x/net 0.63.0, and the latest Playwright, ESLint, typescript-eslint, PostCSS, minimatch and Zag.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **The fortress kit is at 1.0.3.** A BombVault container with a single network interface now hears the other members of the fortress on your network and announces itself there. A member that took a new phrase no longer shows up as gone when its answer got lost on the way back.
