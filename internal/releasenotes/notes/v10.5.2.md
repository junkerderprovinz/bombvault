BombVault 10.5.2 finds the folders of Compose stacks that Dockhand deploys, writes dates the way your region does, and adds true black for OLED screens.

## ✨ Added

- **OLED.** Under **Theme** there is an OLED switch. It gives OLED and AMOLED screens true black whenever the dark theme shows, also when the theme follows the device. Each device keeps its own choice. Thanks to @leonp5 for the idea. ([ArrowLoop #123](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/arrowloop/issues/123))
- **Your device's color as the accent.** The wand at the start of the accent colors takes your device's own color: Material You in the Android app from Android 12 on, and the system color in Firefox and Safari. Thanks to @leonp5 for this one too. ([ArrowLoop #124](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/arrowloop/issues/124))

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Stacks deployed by Dockhand.** Dockhand runs Compose inside its own container, so its stacks name their folder the way Dockhand sees it, `/app/data/stacks/…`. BombVault took that for a folder outside Host Data: the stack's folder was not backed up, and every container of the stack warned about it. BombVault now finds the folder through Dockhand's own mount and backs it up with the stack. Thanks to @manilx for the detailed logs from two servers. ([#416](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/416))
- **A stack that moved to another manager.** When a stack moved, for example from the Compose plugin to Dockhand, and its data moved along, the backup stopped with "its backup folders are not reachable right now (is the appdata share mounted?)", although nothing was unmounted. The backup now stops only while the container still mounts the folders that are missing. If you had chosen its backup folders yourself, the message names the ones that are gone and asks you to choose again. ([#416](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/416))
- **Monitoring apps such as Glances** mount the host's root read-only to read the disks. Their backups no longer warn that `/` lies outside Host Data.
- **Dates in your region's order.** With the English interface most dates came out in American order with AM and PM, so a moment in Portugal read 11/07 05:05 PM. Dates now take their order and clock from your browser's region, and the same moment reads 07/11 17:05. Thanks to @manilx for spotting it. ([#417](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/417))

## ⚡ Improved

- **Follows GlimStone 4.2.1.** Toasts are wider with their button under the message, **Don't ask again** is a real checkbox, each file in a snapshot shows its kind, and a switch that is off is easier to see on the light theme.
- **Readable on every accent color.** Text on an accent color keeps a contrast of at least 4.5:1, whichever color you pick.
