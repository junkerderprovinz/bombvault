BombVault 7.9.2 is a small bugfix release: a rare repository corruption during restarts mid-backup, a flash download that looked stuck after switching tabs, and a smaller flash export.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Restarting BombVault mid-backup could corrupt a restic repository.** Cancelling an in-flight restic process (for example, an update triggered while a backup batch was still uploading) sent SIGKILL, which restic cannot catch or clean up after; a later automatic prune could then remove data a snapshot's tree still referenced, leaving the repository unable to read that snapshot. Cancellation now sends SIGTERM instead, which restic handles as a clean abort with no partial snapshot left behind.
- **A flash zip download looked like it had stopped after switching tabs.** The download's progress lived in the page's own state, which React discards when you navigate away and back, even though the download itself kept running (or had already finished) in the background. Flash downloads now go through the browser's own download manager instead, so they show up correctly no matter what you do in the app while they run.

## ⚡ Improved

- **The flash export drops the previous OS version's boot files.** After an Unraid upgrade, `/boot/previous/` keeps the prior version's kernel around as a rollback safety net, on a real boot drive this can be a third or more of the whole export by itself. It's not part of what a flash export restores (OS, license, array config, shares, network, and plugins are all elsewhere), and Unraid rebuilds it fresh on the next real upgrade regardless, so it's now excluded, shrinking a typical export noticeably and leaving more headroom on smaller USB sticks.
