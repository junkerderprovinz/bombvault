Settings is laid out like KnightLoader now: a column of fourteen pages beside the content, each with its own address, and a search above every page that finds a setting and takes you to it. Pairing has a page of its own.

## 🎨 Changed

### Settings

- **The eight tabs become fourteen pages** in a column beside the content: General, Look, Storage, Retention, Schedules, Containers, Off-site, Cloud access, Notifications, Integrity, Security, Pairing, Integrations and System. Retention, Containers, Cloud access, Security, Pairing and Integrations used to be parts of other tabs.
- **Every page has its own address**, such as `/settings/offsite`, so a page can be bookmarked and the Back button returns to the page before. Settings opens on the page you had open last. Links to the old tabs and to `/jobs` lead to the matching page.
- **Hold a tile and drag it to reorder the column.** The order is saved with your other display settings. The arrow keys, Home and End move between pages.
- **In a narrow window the column shows only the symbols.** It follows **Labels, Tabs** on the Look page too: Symbol shows only the symbols, and Reactive slides the names in when you point at the column. Each tile takes its colour from its place in the column, as the tabs did.
- **Search all settings** sits above every page. It searches page names, card titles, the caption beside each control and the explanations behind the (i), but not the values you have set. Picking a result opens its page, scrolls to the card or row and marks it. When the result is hidden at the moment, because it belongs to the advanced view or to a feature that is off, a note says so.
- **Pairing is always in the column.** Its page has the Receiver, Instances and Pull switches, the three features that work through pairing, and asks for at least one of them to be on. The same switches stay in the Domains card on the General page, with an **Open pairing** link.
- **Restart after backup moves to the Containers page**, below the registries.
- **Every settings card fits beside the column on a phone** down to 320 pixels wide. Long titles, paths and rows wrap instead of running off the side.

## 🌐 Translations

- The page names, the search and the pairing page in all 42 languages.
- The documentation in all 26 languages names the new pages. The translated pages also carry the sections they were missing: SMB and WebDAV without a host mount, Not backed up, the retention preview, the support bundle, pulling from another instance, the sealed recovery kit and remote primary repositories.

## Upgrading

- **Nothing has to be changed.** Settings carry over as they are, and bookmarks to the old tabs lead to the matching page.
- **The column starts in the default order.** Drag a tile to change it.
