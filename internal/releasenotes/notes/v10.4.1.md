BombVault 10.4.1 backs up a VM with Unraid snapshots in full and leaves those snapshots alone during a live backup. A storage location without delete protection can be accepted as it is, every entry offers its own plan on its Settings tab, and stacks are restored from the Restore page. After a prune the local repository is readable for other users right away.

## ✨ Added

- **Accept a storage location without delete protection.** A location that cannot refuse a delete, such as OneDrive, has a switch **Accept without delete protection** on its page, and the Protection Status panel and the Fleet page then show "Delete protection: none, risk accepted" while the section's colour follows its other checks. The backups there can still be deleted, and switching append-only on clears the acceptance. Thanks to @2wheelsdown for the thoughtful request. ([#406](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/406))

## ⚡ Improved

- **Every entry offers its own plan on its Settings tab.** The **Own plan** row showed only while **Own plans per entry** under **Settings, Schedule** was on, and that switch is gone. A plan stored while the switch was off never ran, so the update clears it and names it in the log. Thanks to @manilx for asking where it went. ([#405](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/405))
- **Stacks are restored on the Restore page.** A compose stack has its own **Stacks** card there, and the search finds it by its name or by any of its containers. Thanks to @manilx for pointing out where people look for it. ([#399](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/399))

## 🐛 Fixed

- **A live backup keeps a VM's snapshots.** On a running VM with Unraid snapshots, the live backup merged its temporary overlay all the way down, so every snapshot ended up in the bottom image. It merges only into the image right below, and a VM without snapshots behaves as before. ([#400](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/400))
- **A VM with Unraid snapshots is backed up in full.** BombVault follows each disk's backing chain, backs up every file in it with Unraid's snapshot list, and a restore beside the original points the restored disks at the restored files. A chain that cannot be followed to its end fails the backup and names the file. Thanks to @schwimmbadpirat for the careful report with every check already run. ([#400](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/400))
- **The local repository stays readable after a prune.** Files a prune, tag, pull, section move or delete wrote, and flash or ZFS backups straight to a mounted share, are readable by other users right away instead of the next night. The config repository stays readable by root only. Thanks to @cpsmith516 for tracing the cause through the code. ([#398](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/398))
- **A location without delete protection says so.** The panel called it "not proven yet", which read like the verdict of **Check delete protection**, and it says "Delete protection: none". ([#406](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/406))
- **Opening a repository on a host that is down gives up after 30 seconds** instead of the 15 minutes restic keeps retrying.
- **What's new lists Gotify** among the 10.4.0 changes.
