BombVault 10.6.1 lights everything under the pointer in your accent color and sends your questions to GitHub or Discord.

## 🎨 Design

- **Follows GlimStone 4.5.4.** Buttons, rows, menus, tiles and tabs light up in one hover color made from your accent, so a button and the row beside it no longer glow in two different shades. The glyph tile of a Restore row fills with the accent under the pointer, like every other row's.
- **Accent text reads well on every surface.** In the light theme the accent's text color sits a little deeper and keeps a contrast of at least 4.5:1 on cards as well as on white. In the dark theme blue's text is a little lighter.
- **A clearer focus ring.** The ring around whatever the keyboard is on is drawn solid in the accent's text color, for a single accent and for every rainbow position.
- **Placeholders you can read.** The hint inside an empty field keeps 4.5:1 on every field color.

## ⚡ Improved

- **Help goes to GitHub and Discord.** The Help card under **Info** names both, and a Discord button takes the mail button's place. It opens BombVault's forum on Discord.
- **Keys work as in the rest of the family.** Only Space flips a checkbox, Home and End on a closed dropdown open its list at that end, and a typed letter jumps to the next matching option in a list or to a row of an open action menu.
- **The GlimStone version under Info** opens GlimStone's newest release notes.
- **Fresh building blocks.** OpenTelemetry 1.47.0 with a security fix, x/crypto 0.59.0, a fresh Node image, Gradle 9.9.1 and Bouncy Castle 1.87 for the Android app, and the latest jsdom, minimatch, typescript-eslint and Vite.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **A button's label stays readable under the pointer.** With a pale accent on the dark theme, or a mid-tone accent on the light one, the hover could take a label below 4.5:1. It now keeps that contrast for every color you pick.
- **A button that shows its words on hover stays under the pointer.** It grew while its words appeared, so in a row that wraps it could slide away, close and open again. It now holds its room and the words open inside it.
- **The fortress kit is at 1.0.1.** An app holding the phrase could announce an address that sent BombVault's direct call to another page of a host on your network. The call now always goes where it belongs.
- **Fortress members last seen in the same second as a phrase change** are now marked as still on the old phrase.

## 🌐 Translations

- **One help sentence in all 42 languages**, worded the same as in every other app of the family, and French readers are addressed with tu.
