BombVault works on a phone. Below 48rem every page has a layout made for the width, with a bottom bar in place of the sidebar, and nothing the desktop can do is missing. The desktop layout is unchanged. The phone shell, the dashboard and the Containers, VMs, Recovery and Folders pages are the work of [@CatFoxVoyager](https://github.com/CatFoxVoyager). Thank you!

## ✨ Added

### The phone layout

- **A bottom bar** with Dashboard, Containers, Folders and Recovery, and **More** for the rest: Anomalies, VMs, Flash, ZFS, Self-Backup and Instances as far as they are switched on, Settings, the Simple/Advanced view switch and sign-out. The order follows the sidebar ([#244](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/pull/244)). A dot on More shows open critical or warning anomalies ([#265](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/pull/265)).
- **Every page fits a phone from 320px up:** Dashboard ([#244](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/pull/244), [#269](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/pull/269)), Containers ([#245](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/pull/245)), VMs ([#248](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/pull/248)), Recovery ([#253](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/pull/253)), Folders ([#254](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/pull/254)), Flash ([#263](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/pull/263)), Self-Backup ([#264](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/pull/264)), Anomalies ([#265](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/pull/265)), ZFS ([#266](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/pull/266)), Instances ([#267](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/pull/267)) and Settings ([#268](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/pull/268)).
- **Containers and VMs as cards.** Each opens a detail view with its schedule, backups and actions. Search, filters and sort sit in a toolbar above the list, and long lists load more on request.
- **The phone dashboard** shows the next backup, the recent runs, storage, anomalies, ransomware protection (in the advanced view, as on the desktop), protection status, what is not backed up, and the activity log. While a critical or warning anomaly is open, the anomalies card comes first. **Backup Everything** sits above the bottom bar.
- **Run details open full screen**, and questions such as "Delete this backup?" come up as a sheet at the bottom of the screen with both answers in reach.
- **Controls grow to finger size under a touch pointer**: settings tabs, number field arrows, row chips, small links and the password eye.
- **The bottom bar has its own label setting** in the **Labels** card under **Settings, General**, beside the ones for buttons, the sidebar and tabs.
- **`get_storage_stats` reports free and total space.** For every repository of a domain it gives the used, free and total space of the disk or remote it lives on, and the weeks until the primary one is full.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **A lock left by an interrupted run no longer blocks checks and prunes.** Process IDs start from the bottom again after a container restart, so the lock of a restic killed mid-run often names an ID that is in use once more, and restic counted it as held. When that ID was 1, restic's probe of the lock's owner killed BombVault itself. BombVault removes such a lock from its local repositories before it unlocks them, keeps every lock a restic it started still holds, and ignores the signal restic probes with.
- **The "Remove" chips for hooks under Settings, Schedules did nothing** at any width. They are buttons now.
- **Repository switches printed their labels twice** under Settings, Paths & Storage, and a button on the Instances page was cut off at 768px.
- **Long names and addresses stay inside their cards**: socket paths in a failed database dump, dataset names, MCP key names, passkey names and off-site addresses wrap instead of widening the page.

## 🌐 Translations

## Upgrading

- **Nothing has to be changed.** Settings, schedules and repositories carry over as they are, and no setting or environment variable was removed or renamed.
