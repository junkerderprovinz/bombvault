Backup processes can now be given a memory limit, and the Instances page keeps servers and phones that are switched off instead of dropping them.

## ✨ Added

- **Memory limit per backup.** Settings, System, Performance has a new field, **Memory limit per backup (MiB)**. It sets a soft limit for each backup process, so a large backup slows down instead of being killed for running out of memory. 0 sets none, which is how backups ran before.
- **Instances that are away stay in the group.** A server that is shut down or a phone with the app closed keeps its card on the Instances page and its row on the Pairing page, marked **Inactive** in grey with when it was last seen, until you remove it. When it is back it is the same card again, never a second one. **Remove** works on such a card whichever modules are switched on. The Android app shows an instance that is away the same way, and it now also lists the instances of the group it never met itself, such as one that was shut down when the phone joined.

## 🎨 Design

- **Instance cards are all one size.** Every card of the grid is as wide and as tall as the tallest one, and the logo sits at the top beside the name, at one size on every card.
- **Names are shown in full.** A long name on a card wraps, the badges move below it, and the kind of instance has a line of its own. In the picture of the group, names wrap too instead of being cut short.
- **Every BombVault in the picture of the group shows the logo,** this server, the other instances and the phones, with a small round badge that says whether it is a server or a phone.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Right after a restart, the other instances showed as Not connected in red** until the group was back. A card now says **Checking** in grey until then, and its node in the picture is not dimmed.
