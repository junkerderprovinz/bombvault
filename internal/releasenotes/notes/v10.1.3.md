A patch for What's new and the widget: an update shows a card for every release since the one you had, every new feature has its green dot, and the widget shows a ZFS replica as replicated.

## ✨ Added

- **One card per release in What's new.** After an update the window lists every release since the one you had, newest first. Each card shows its release notes and the changes that release brought, each with **Go there** where a page shows it. A change that no page shows, such as how a backup behaves, has its dot in the window only and counts as seen once you close it. ([#354](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/354))

## 🐛 Fixed

- **10.1 had no green dots.** The new features of 10.1.0 to 10.1.2 were missing from the list of changes, so every dot stayed grey after the update. They have their dots now, and every new feature of a release gets one. ([#354](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/354))
- **The widget showed a ZFS replica as a backup.** Its replica lines read "backed up", and ZFS, Full backup, Appdata.Backup imports and the events of the backup key had no name of their own. Every line now names its section, as the activity log does, and reads the way the log puts it. ([#355](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/355))

## 🌐 Translations

- **The new texts in all 42 languages**, and the documentation of What's new in all 26.
