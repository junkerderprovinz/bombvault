The About card says what this workshop is and how to support it, in every language. An entry that is no longer installed can come off the schedule without losing its backups, and BombVault finds its own container whatever the container's hostname is.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Not-installed entries have the "Include in schedule" switch** ([#232](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/232)). A renamed or removed container stayed on the schedule and recorded a skip on every run, and its card offered no way out except deleting all of its backups. Switch it off and the skips stop, while the backups stay restorable. The VM page works the same way.
- **"Exclude all" reaches entries that are no longer installed, and "Include all" no longer does.** On the VM page, "Include all" used to put every deleted VM back on the schedule.
- **The "Not installed (backups only)" heading no longer covers its hint.** The heading badge is a notch made to sit on the edge of a card, and here a line of text sat under it instead. The hint is in the badge's (i) now.
- **Deleting all backups of a VM whose local repository is gone removes its entry** instead of failing every time.
- **BombVault recognises its own container with a pinned hostname or on the host network.** It used to look itself up by hostname, which only works while Docker's default short ID is left in place. The Unraid template, the compose file and the TrueNAS app all pin the hostname, and host networking replaces it, so BombVault could miss itself or mistake another container for itself. It now reads its container ID from the mount table.
- **A tooltip no longer stays on screen after Prune** ([#243](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/243)). A tooltip now closes when its button is disabled under it, and only keyboard focus opens one, so a dialog handing focus back after Cancel no longer does either.
- **The Unraid template mounts Host Data as Read/Write - Slave.** Unraid turned the old `rw,rslave` into plain `rw`, so a share or disk mounted after BombVault started stayed invisible to it.
- **Test connection no longer reports a server it cannot reach as reachable but not initialised** ([#246](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/246)). restic words a missing repository and a failed connection almost the same way, and BombVault took both for an empty destination. A mistyped host name, a refused port or a server that is down now fail the test with restic's own reason, such as "no such host". The same mix-up let a remote off-site repository that was down show up as holding no snapshots.
- **The sidebar scrolls when the window is too short for it** ([#247](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/247)). On a short window or a phone, Advanced view and Settings at the bottom of the sidebar were cut off and out of reach.
- **The light theme's accent ink is derived from the accent instead of pinned to gold.** It was a fixed value chosen when nothing here could derive a contrast-safe ink from an arbitrary colour, so a custom accent left golden text sitting on a surface in a completely different hue - about 160 degrees away for a blue accent. It is a mix now, measured across all eight presets rather than taken from the design language's own number: the worst case reads 5.13:1 where the fixed gold read 4.74:1.
- **The PayPal button opens a donation page rather than a handle.** It says who is being paid, carries a sentence about what the money does, offers three amounts and a free one, takes a card without a PayPal account, and has a box for making it monthly.

## 🎨 Design

- **The About card carries the house text.** One paragraph about what this is and one about supporting it, in all forty-two languages. The two used to say slightly different things in this app than in its siblings, so two of them side by side read as two projects with two different people behind them.
- **The crypto button wears the bare Bitcoin letterform**, not the coin disc. At 16px beside a coffee cup and a PayPal P, a disc hands the eye the shape of the ground instead of the shape of the letter. The disc keeps the coin tiles inside the donation window, where the mark is large and telling eight logos apart is the whole job.
- **The mail button is called "Email" and wears an envelope.** It says where the button goes rather than describing what the reader is about to do, which every button on the card could claim, and the envelope draws the thing rather than the act.
- **A button takes the colour of the mark it carries.** The mail button's mark follows the accent, so the button followed it too instead of turning red on hover.
- **Button labels stopped losing the bottom of every descender.** The label box hides its overflow and had a line height of exactly 1, so at 0.875rem every p, g, y and j was cut about 1.7px short. Nothing moves: the button keeps its own height and the extra leading splits evenly.
- **The download buttons in the README carry a band of light that crosses the whole row**, one row at a time down the page, and it is the same band the donation row runs rather than a second effect that happens to look similar.
- **A not-installed card has one removal button, in its top right corner**, on the container page and the VM page alike. It reads "Delete all backups" while the entry has backups and "Remove entry" once it has none. The two pages used to disagree: the container card could only delete the backups, the VM card could only remove the entry, whatever state the entry was in. Removing just the entry while backups exist is no longer offered, because those backups would then disappear from the page.

## ⚡ Improved

## 🧹 Housekeeping

- **`DELETE /api/containers/{name}` removes a container's entry without touching its snapshots**, the counterpart of the existing VM route. Both refuse while the container or VM is installed, and while a backup or restore of that kind is running.
- **The VM page uses the container page's schedule switch** instead of keeping its own copy.
- **The TrueNAS app follows the catalog's conventions** and no longer asks for BombVault's own container name.
- **Dependency updates**, among them a security fix in the OpenTelemetry trace exporter that the Docker client pulls in.
- **Comments, log lines and error messages read more plainly.** Apart from small wording changes in the test notification and the weekly digest, BombVault behaves exactly as before.

## Upgrading

On Unraid, an install keeps the Access Mode it was set up with, and older installs have Host Data on plain Read/Write. BombVault then misses a share or disk that mounts after it has started, such as a remote share under `/mnt/remotes` at boot, until it is restarted. In the Docker tab, edit BombVault, open Host Data, set Access Mode to "Read/Write - Slave" and apply. New installs get this from the template.
