BombVault 7.9.1 is a small bugfix release: a dashboard display glitch during long off-site uploads, and flash zip downloads that failed to extract in the official Unraid USB Creator.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Flash zip downloads now extract cleanly in the official Unraid USB Creator.** restic's own zip writer stores every entry uncompressed, which the USB Creator's unzip implementation cannot reliably extract past a certain point (verified against a real device down to the byte level). The download is now recompressed on the way out, and a handful of non-Unraid artifacts that have no business in a boot-drive restore (a stray `.git` checkout, Windows' `System Volume Information`, fsck recovery dumps, 0-byte `.bmp` remnants) are dropped along the way. ([#136](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/136))
- **The dashboard's "replicating" activity line no longer disappears during a long off-site upload.** `restic copy` has no incremental progress output, so a replication slower than 15 seconds looked like it had silently vanished from the dashboard, even though it was still running correctly in the background. It now sends a periodic heartbeat so the line stays visible for the whole upload. ([#134](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/134))
