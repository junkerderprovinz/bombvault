## 🐛 Fixed

- **The page gutter was this app's number instead of the house's.** v8.6.5 moved the rail off the window edge and made it a card, and set the gutter to 1.5rem because that is what the content padding happened to be. The design language says the gutter is one number for the whole house; it had not said which, so the two apps that follow the rule picked differently. Side by side that was the whole impression rather than a detail: at 1.5rem the rail sits further from the edge and, losing half a rem at the top and the bottom each, reads as a visibly smaller rail than the identical one beside it. It is 1rem now, and the language names the number so the next app does not have to guess. Measured after the change, both apps live at the same window size: the rail is at the same position, the same 224 by 868, the same radius, the same distance to the content, in both.

- The content keeps its own 1.5rem of padding, which is a separate distance and unchanged. Between the rail and the first card the two simply add up.
