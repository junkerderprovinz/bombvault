BombVault 10.6.4 ships restic 0.20.0 with two security fixes for restores. It also clears the open scanner findings for the restic copy inside the image.

## ⚡ Improved

- **restic 0.20.0.** When a restore sets owners by name and a user or group name cannot be resolved, restic falls back to the numeric owner from the snapshot instead of handing the file to root. A restore also refuses snapshots with duplicate or out-of-order names, and the index needs less memory.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Snapshot holds are counted safely.** BombVault reads the number of holds ZFS reports on a snapshot at its full 64-bit size and no longer narrows it to a smaller integer.
