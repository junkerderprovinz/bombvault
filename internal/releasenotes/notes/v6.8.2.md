Cron expressions as a schedule type, and log dates now follow your region.

## Added

- **Cron schedules (#107).** Every schedule (backups, off-site, drills, tamper tests, digest) can now use a raw 5-field cron expression — e.g. `0 */6 * * *` for every six hours. The schedule builder gets a "Cron" mode with live validation, a preview of the next three run times, and clickable examples. Existing preset schedules are untouched; switching to Cron prefills the equivalent expression.

## Fixed

- **Log dates follow your region, not the UI language (#104).** The activity-log date was ordered by the app language, so an English UI showed US-style "07/24" even in Europe. It now follows your browser/system locale — Portugal sees "24/07" with the English UI — and searching by the displayed form or an ISO date keeps working.
