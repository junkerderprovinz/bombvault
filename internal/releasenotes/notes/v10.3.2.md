BombVault 10.3.2 lets an exclude turn off the warning about data outside Host Data, and an exclude line `/` can no longer empty a backup.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **An exclude turns off the warning about data outside Host Data.** A container with data BombVault cannot reach, most often an anonymous Docker volume under `/var/lib/docker/volumes`, turned every one of its backups orange, and nothing made it stop. Add the folder's path inside the container, or a folder above it, to the container's exclude patterns, and the warning leaves that folder out. The preview under the field says the folder sits outside Host Data and that the line turns off its warning. ([#377](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/377))
- **An exclude line `/` no longer empties the backup.** A container exclude of `/`, or a line such as `*` or `/**` that matches every path, went to restic as it was. The backup then held nothing but empty folders and still reported success. BombVault ignores such a line, and the preview under the field says it would exclude everything.
