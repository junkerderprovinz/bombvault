Findings that come from history read back after an upgrade now point at the right backup, and a finding can show which folders changed.

## ✨ Added

- **Compare with the backup before.** Findings about how much a container, VM or folder set holds or stores have a **Compare with the backup before** button. It compares the last good backup with the first affected one and lists the folders where files were removed, added or changed. If nearly all of the change is in a search index, a cache or thumbnails, it says so. Apps rebuild those folders on their own, and excluding such a folder keeps the rebuilds out of your backups and out of the findings.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Findings from history read back after an upgrade are dated to the backup they began on.** BombVault reads that history in one go. A source that had already shrunk several backups earlier was dated to the newest backup, and the last good backup it offered was the one just before, which could be affected too. The finding now starts at the first affected backup and offers the one before it for a restore. Reported in [#281](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/281).
- **A finding names the backup it began on.** An opened finding shows its first affected backup. First seen, when BombVault noticed the problem, moves to the details, because after an upgrade that can be days after the backup.
