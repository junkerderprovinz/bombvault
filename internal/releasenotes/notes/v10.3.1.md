BombVault 10.3.1 can keep the newest ZFS replica snapshots on the source too, and an orange line in the log says why it is orange.

## ✨ Added

- **Keep replica snapshots on the source too.** The Replica card of a ZFS entry has a new field, **Keep on this server**. It sets how many of the newest replica snapshots stay on the source after each replica, next to the ones the target keeps, so going back to one of them is a quick `zfs rollback` with nothing to transfer. The newest keeps its hold as the base of the next transfer. The older ones have none, so you can roll back to them or destroy them by hand. 0 or 1 keeps only the newest, as before. ([#359](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/discussions/359))

## 🐛 Fixed

- **An orange line in the log says why it is orange.** A backup that went through but left something out, such as data outside Host Data or files it could not read, turned its line orange without a word. The line names what was left out and the folders or files behind it, every note of the run in your language. ([#377](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/bombvault/issues/377))
