## ✨ Added

- **Sign in through a sign-in service.** Under Settings › Security › Sign-in services you can add Pocket ID, authentik, Authelia, Kanidm, Keycloak or Zitadel next to your password. Enter its address, client ID and secret, run the test sign-in, and the sign-in page gets a button for it. For now BombVault has to sit at the root of its address for this, since under a path such as `https://example.com/bombvault/` the service can't send you back yet.
- **The address of this instance.** The new Address card under Settings › Security keeps where you open BombVault from outside, such as `https://backup.example`. Passkeys and sign-in services go by it, and you can leave it empty while you only use BombVault at home.
- **Sessions you can see.** The Sessions card lists every browser that is signed in, and you can sign any of them out, or all the others at once.
- **The password check on changes.** When you signed in more than ten minutes ago, BombVault asks for your password in a small window before a change such as a new passkey or sign-in service, and then carries the change out. You can turn the question off.
- **Accounts.** People who come in through a sign-in service get an account with the role you pick for newcomers, and the Accounts card lets you change the role, block an account or remove it. Blocking, removing or demoting an account revokes the MCP keys, API tokens and OAuth grants it made, and a phone it approved stays out until someone approves it again.
- **Phones of the group always sign in.** A phone you let into the group gets its session without a switch to turn on first. A new phone still waits under Settings › Pairing until you press Approve there. A password reset ends every phone's session, and the phones sign in again by themselves. To keep one phone out, remove it on the Instances page.

## 🎨 Design

- **The sign-in page and the Security tab come from GlimStone 4.9.2.** The page has at most two buttons, Sign in and the sign-in service when one is on, with Forgot password? as a plain link below them. Your browser offers a saved passkey right in the password field.
- **The sign-in lists look like your connections.** Passkeys, sessions, sign-in services and accounts stand in the same rows as the cloud connections under Storage locations: the service's logo or the kind's glyph on a tile, the name over a quiet line, and the buttons at the end. A long passkey or device name wraps on a phone, so two that start alike stay apart.
- **A sign-in service's switch speaks for itself.** It sits at the end of the name's line, above Test sign-in, Edit and Remove, and no On badge repeats it.
- **The password question sits in the password card,** as a switch under the password.
- **Empty cards look like an empty list.** Without a password, the password card says "No password yet" over Set a password, and two-factor that is off says "Not set up yet" over Set up, the way the passkeys card does without a passkey.
- **A tidier Security tab.** The Android app card with its pairing switch is gone, since phones always pair, and the Instances card drops its line pointing to Pairing.

## ⚡ Improved

- **Nothing to set up again.** On the first start BombVault takes over your password, two-factor authentication with its recovery codes, your passkeys and the open sessions, so nobody has to sign in again.
- **Staying signed in.** A sign-in lasts 30 days, or 7 without a visit.
- **The sign-in comes from ParleyPort,** the kit your other fortress apps sign in with too.
- **The sign-in settings stay with a real sign-in.** A paired phone and a page opened from another app of your fortress can't change the password, passkeys, sessions or sign-in services.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Opening a member right after a join.** A member is off the relay for a moment while it reconnects, and opening it in that gap ended in an error although it was back right after. BombVault keeps trying for a moment before it gives up.
