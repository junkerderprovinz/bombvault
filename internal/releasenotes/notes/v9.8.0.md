BombVault's companions get a page of their own. **Settings, Apps** lists ParleyPort, your own relay for paired instances on different networks, and the BombVault Widget for the Unraid dashboard, each with the buttons the README has.

## ✨ Added

- **Settings, Apps.** A new page beside Integrations with a card for each companion. ParleyPort's card offers its Unraid template, copies the Docker command that starts it, and leads to its repository and to the relay settings under Pairing. The BombVault Widget's card offers its template and its repository, and installs or removes the plugin over the host SSH connection. That part sat on the dashboard widget card under Integrations before; the embeddable activity log stays there.

## 🎨 Design

- **Every glyph follows one standard, shared with KnightLoader.** Each meaning has one drawing, the same in both apps, and no two meanings share one. Prune gets a shredder of its own instead of the bin that Delete wears. Schedules, Notifications and Apps in the Settings rail come from the same set as the rest of the rail. Security keeps a shield: a plain one, beside the checked shield of Integrity.

## ⚡ Improved

- **Your own relay is ParleyPort.** The relay card under Pairing names the ParleyPort container from the Community Apps as one of the two ways to run your own relay, in every language and in the documentation. It is the same relay as before, now published on its own so BombVault and KnightLoader share one.
- **The project relay has a new address**, `parleyport.halleluja.design`, and its card says it runs ParleyPort. Nothing to change on your side: the project relay was never stored, so every instance dials the new name after the update, and the old one keeps answering for a few releases. A DNS filter that allows only the old name needs the new one too.
