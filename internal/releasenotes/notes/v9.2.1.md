Pairing asks first whether this is your first instance. Answering yes on two instances made two separate groups that never found each other; now the second one is asked to answer no, and a group that stays alone says after a minute how to join the other one.

The Fleet tab shows every instance of the group as a card, this one included, and says for each whether it is connected. The tabs in Settings and Instances are separate tabs again.

## 🎨 Changed

### Pairing

- **The tab opens with one sentence: connect all your instances with a 12-word phrase.** An (i) beside it says the words make the key that never leaves your instances.
- **The phrase card asks "Is this your first instance?"** with two large answers. **Yes, create phrase** starts the group and shows the twelve words with a Copy button and the path to take on the other instance. **No, I already have the words** opens the field for the words of your first instance.
- **The step cards follow the question**: First instance, Every other one, Done. They keep to one or two short sentences each; the warning not to answer yes again sits in the second one's (i).
- **The field for the words takes a paste as it comes**: one word per line, numbered, or separated by commas. Twelve numbered slots fill as you type, a word that is not on the list is named with its position right away, and Pair stays off until all twelve are known words. Mistyped or swapped words that are all on the list are still caught by the checksum when you press Pair.
- **The badge says Paired only once another instance is there.** Before that it says New group right after creating one, Searching in the first minute after joining, and Still alone after that.
- **Still alone after a minute, the card says what to do.** If both instances created a phrase, it shows the two steps: leave this group, then enter the other instance's words. The other instance stays as it is. It also offers to show the phrase for when nothing was entered over there yet, and without a relay it points at the other network.
- **One line in the phrase card shows the relay**: connected, not reachable, or no relay. When the relay is not reachable and nobody has shown up, the card lists what to check: outgoing HTTPS on port 443 to relay.halleluja.design, a firewall or DNS filter, or for your own relay whether it runs and its address is right.
- Pairing still needs a login password. Without one, both answers are disabled and the card says why.
- **The relay card starts with what a relay is for**: it connects BombVault instances even across different networks. Its top shows Connected, Not connected or No relay. The picture of the route, what the relay can see and the encryption note open under **How does it work?**, so the card itself stays short.

### Fleet

- **Every instance of the group gets a card with the BombVault logo**, this one first and marked This instance. Each card shows Connected or Not connected and the protection scorecard. It no longer says whether a member is reached directly or through the relay.
- **No more Poll now button.** While the tab is open it checks every 20 seconds who is connected, and it fetches a connected member's scorecard by itself once the cached one is older than 15 minutes. Check now and the monitoring switch are under Details, Offer storage and Remove stay on the card.

### Settings and Instances

- **The tabs at the top are separate tabs again**, each as wide as the longest name, instead of one groove across the page. On a phone they still span the width.
- **The switches that turn on the Instances tabs carry the tabs' names**: Receiver, Fleet and Pull, instead of Receiver dashboard, Fleet view and Pull from other instances.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **A VM's Graceful/Live switch keeps its size when you click it.** While it saved, the switch lost track of how wide its segments are and came back narrower.

## 🌐 Translations

- The new pairing, relay and Fleet texts in all 42 languages.

## Upgrading

- Nothing to do. An instance that is already in a group counts as having joined at the update. If no other instance is reachable then, it shows the Still alone hint after a minute.
